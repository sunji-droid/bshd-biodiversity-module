— Journalist Training Module • Botswana

Stronger reporting on biodiversity loss, built in from the first draft, not added at the end.

Biodiversity loss, environmental degradation, and human wildlife conflict are still under reported across Botswana's media. This module gives working journalists the knowledge, sourcing, and story development skills to cover it with depth, from the first draft.

How to Navigate This Training Curriculum

When you enter the course (`by clicking the Enter Course Dashboard or Start Unit 1 buttons above`), you will be taken directly into our reading interface where the Module Navigation Side Bar sits cleanly on the left side of your screen.

Inside that side bar, all 13 sections and 5 training units are cleanly wrapped inside collapsible category buttons (`📍 Course Platform & Overview` and `📚 Curriculum Units 1–5`). You can open any unit right inside the side bar at any time without screen clutter!
